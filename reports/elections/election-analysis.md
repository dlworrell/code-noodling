## Method and limits

Risk percentages are transparent heuristic bands, not statistical election forecasts. Ballot batches are not assumed to be random samples.

Remaining contest votes are estimated by scaling current contest ballots to each source's configured expected final ballot count. Missing forecasts produce an Unknown assessment.

When the available file is only a county slice of a multicounty or statewide race, the report marks it noncontrolling and does not treat it as the official outcome forecast.
