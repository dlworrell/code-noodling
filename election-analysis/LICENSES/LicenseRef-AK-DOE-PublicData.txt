Copyright text: State of Alaska, Division of Elections

The files this identifier covers are public election results published by the
State of Alaska, Division of Elections, retrieved from
https://www.elections.alaska.gov/. The publisher's terms of use are UNKNOWN:
not reviewed, and no license is stated in the files or responses retrieved.
Donovan Worrell claims no copyright in these files and grants no license to
them. They are kept as source evidence for the election reports in this
repository, unmodified except as each election's SOURCES.json notes (for
example, API JSON trimmed to results and gzipped, or converted to plain-text
CSV).

The precinct-to-borough crosswalk (data/crosswalks/alaska/) is derived from
the Division's 2023 election precinct layer and from 2020 Census TIGER/Line
blocks. The Census input is a U.S. Government work; only derived precinct
identifiers and population counts are stored, not the precinct geometry. As a
file derived from public data it takes this identifier (owner decision
2026-10-23). Sources and SHA-256 of the downloads are in
data/crosswalks/alaska/README.md.

Applies to: election-analysis/data/elections/alaska/*/input/ and
election-analysis/data/elections/alaska/*/raw/ (statewide and every borough
and census area, all derived from the Division's precinct results), and
election-analysis/data/crosswalks/alaska/*.csv
