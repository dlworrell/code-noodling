Original public election records published by the Orange County Registrar of Voters
and its officially linked results vendor. Copyright and other applicable rights
remain with their respective publishers. Public availability is not represented
as an open license or a grant of rights by this project. These unmodified records
are retained as attributed, hash-bound factual audit evidence. Reusers must assess
applicable permissions themselves. Project-authored readers and metadata have
separate Apache-2.0 notices. No voter-level records are included.

Source: https://ocvote.gov/data/election-results-archives
