The files this identifier covers are public election results published by the
Maine Secretary of State (Bureau of Corporations, Elections and Commissions),
retrieved from
https://www.maine.gov/sos/elections-voting/election-results-data. The
publisher's terms of use are UNKNOWN: not reviewed, and no license is stated
in the files or responses retrieved. Donovan Worrell claims no copyright in
these files and grants no license to them. They are kept as source evidence
for the election reports in this repository, unmodified except as each
election's SOURCES.json notes (for example, API JSON trimmed to results and
gzipped, or converted to plain-text CSV).

Applies to: election-analysis/data/elections/maine/*/input/ and
election-analysis/data/elections/maine/*/raw/ (statewide and every county,
all derived from the Secretary of State's results workbooks)
