The files this identifier covers are public election results published by the
Snohomish County Auditor's elections office (Snohomish County, Washington),
retrieved from the Washington results site https://results.votewa.gov/ (county
results API). The publisher's terms of use are UNKNOWN: not reviewed, and no
license is stated in the files or responses retrieved. Donovan Worrell claims
no copyright in these files and grants no license to them. They are kept as
source evidence for the election reports in this repository, unmodified except
as each election's SOURCES.json notes (for example, API JSON trimmed to
results and gzipped, or converted to plain-text CSV).

Applies to: election-analysis/data/elections/washington/*/input/snohomish-county/ and
election-analysis/data/elections/washington/*/raw/snohomish-county/
