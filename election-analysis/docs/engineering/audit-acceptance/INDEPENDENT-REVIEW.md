# Independent statistical review brief

Authoritative audit: [#45](https://github.com/dlworrell/code-noodling/issues/45).
Open children: [EA-10 #55](https://github.com/dlworrell/code-noodling/issues/55),
[EA-13 #58](https://github.com/dlworrell/code-noodling/issues/58).
This is the implementer's original review packet. The separate AI review and
independent repair review were completed on October 25, 2026; their unchanged
reports, exact scope and accepted dispositions are linked in
[INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-ACCEPTANCE.md](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-ACCEPTANCE.md).
The questions below remain the original brief, rather than a reviewer verdict.

The reviewer should independently read the original audit/acceptance criteria,
source records, ADR-004 through ADR-008, first protocols/seals, complete score and
exclusion ledgers, source qualification, test/CI evidence and current issue bodies.
Record the exact reviewed commit and artifact hashes. A reviewer must not rely
solely on the implementer's summary or green tests. No live model promotion,
parameter retuning, publisher contact or issue mutation is part of this brief.

Answer each question with supporting evidence and PASS / PARTIAL / BLOCKED:

1. Are reported score targets, ties, RCV stages and county/whole-contest scopes
   precise and consistent with the actual scored outcomes?
2. Do prediction inputs exclude reserved final results and later corrections?
   How strong are the dated publisher archive and amended-form availability
   claims? Distinguish phase isolation, qualified reconstruction and published
   historical forecasts without imposing an unstated cryptographic requirement.
3. Does batch classification fail closed on unknown/correction/recount/transfer
   deltas? Does the conditional OC increment comparison stay distinct from a
   verified new-ballot likelihood? Are disjoint weights exposed without claiming
   independent sample sizes or fitted calibration?
4. Are acceptance, challenge overlap, geographic/pair participation, arrivals
   and ballot-versus-card units supported or explicitly conditional? Does the new
   manifest actually fill a required field, or merely narrow the data request?
5. Are grouping, no-fitting discipline, baselines, losses, calibration diagnostics,
   unavailable intervals and dependence limits represented faithfully? More
   snapshots or sensitivities must not create fictitious independent samples.
6. Can source/format qualification, sealed predictions and scores reproduce from
   their exact hashes and original runtime? Do modern parser qualification and
   original-runtime reproduction have correctly separate claims?
7. Apply the original issue criteria faithfully. Separate mandatory work from
   later stronger acquisition requests. Determine whether the explicitly
   documented unvalidated result satisfies a permitted failure outcome or whether
   specific criteria still prevent closure. Do not either raise or lower the
   standard merely to obtain a zero issue count.

Return actionable findings with severity, exact file/line/source references,
minimum fixes and a closure recommendation separately for #55, #58 and #45.
Explicitly state what evidence supports any use beyond conditional experimental
scenarios. The independent reviewer may conclude that issues must remain open.
