## What the repair establishes

One shared qualification step now binds a contest to its actual immediately preceding source snapshot **before** history filtering or deduplication. It checks source/contest/stage/version identity, complete coverage, increasing source/count evidence, the entire candidate inventory, all candidate changes, both ballot denominators, endpoint accounting and known over/under counters. Unknown counters stay unknown. Observed increments cannot exceed the reported ballot increment.

An explicit `new_ballots` annotation remains necessary; a positive transition alone does not create it. Duplicate bytes, missing/incompatible immediate predecessors and insufficient prior evidence supply no latest likelihood. Contradictory annotated transitions additionally quarantine the scenario and expose a failed `batch_annotation_consistent` gate/reasons while retaining descriptive counts. Pair and majority calculations consume the same qualified transition rather than independently subtracting filtered histories. The evidence exposes both source hashes.

This directly repairs both initial defects. The distinction between an inadequate predecessor and a contradictory annotation is reasonable: the former may retain a separately eligible cumulative-only scenario; the latter cannot authorize the annotated batch/scenario. No empirical independence or correction-free publisher assertion is inferred merely from nonnegative arithmetic.

I independently exercised **28 cases**, including all five original failures. They cover negative third-candidate changes, decreasing denominators, removed/added candidates, increments exceeding ballots, both pair and majority paths, three-snapshot duplicates, omitted/incompatible immediate predecessors, decreasing/changed-availability counters, insufficient prior coverage/clocks, explicit unknown/correction/transfer/recount annotations, and compatible positive controls. All rejected transitions contribute zero latest votes. Contradictory cases withhold probabilities; duplicate/inadequate-history cases can retain the supported cumulative fallback.

Positive controls independently verify the capped disjoint arithmetic. For prior A800/B600/C100 and current A900/B650/C150 (1,500→1,700 ballots), the winner pair receives 150 latest votes with alpha `1 + (600/1400)*300 + 50` and beta `1 + (800/1400)*300 + 100`. The majority event receives 200 latest votes with alpha **241**, beta **261** and zero reused overlap. No cap or probability formula changed.

The user guide now states the original Measure 2 archive's 737-vote margin alongside the later 743-vote recount, and distinguishes first choices from final RCV allocation. Markdown, HTML and social publication now say **298 source representations of contests**, with overlapping jurisdictions potentially represented separately. These changes address ISR-003 without asserting new source authority or validation.
