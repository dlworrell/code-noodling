The files this identifier covers are a public dataset published by King County
Elections (King County, Washington). King County Elections states no license
for the dataset. Donovan Worrell claims no copyright in these files and grants
no license to them. They are kept unmodified, except as noted per file below,
as source evidence for the election reports in this repository.

Applies to: election-analysis/data/elections/washington/*/input/king-county/
(formerly election-data/input/king-county/)

Files are listed below under the paths they were first committed at. Their
current paths, and every later snapshot, are recorded in each election's
SOURCES.json.
