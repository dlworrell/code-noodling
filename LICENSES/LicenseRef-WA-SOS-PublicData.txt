Status: Draft (PRM-0011 Slice 0). Recorded licensing decision; REUSE headers
and the remaining license files are added in Slice 1.

Copyright text: Washington Secretary of State

The files this identifier covers are a public dataset published by the
Washington Secretary of State, Elections Division, through the official
results site https://results.votewa.gov/. The Secretary of State states no
license for the dataset. Donovan Worrell claims no copyright in these files
and grants no license to them. They are kept unmodified as source evidence for
the election reports in this repository.

Applies to: election-analysis/data/elections/washington/*/input/washington/ and
election-analysis/data/elections/washington/*/raw/washington/
(formerly election-data/input/washington/)

Files are listed below under the paths they were first committed at. Their
current paths, and every later snapshot, are recorded in each election's
SOURCES.json.

The results site lists each election's downloadable reports through
https://results.votewa.gov/results/public/api/elections/washington/<YYYYMMDD>
and serves them from
https://results.votewa.gov/cdn/results/<jurisdiction id>/<report file name>.
The Washington jurisdiction id is 01000000-f977-1a83-1ed4-08ddd69e9e69. Report
files are overwritten in place as results are updated, so an earlier snapshot
cannot be re-downloaded after it is replaced.
